# Writing

Read this before writing any content, labels, messages or emails. These rules apply to visible content, code comments, commit messages, READMEs, documentation, email templates and legal pages. They do not apply to the delivery summary or chat messages after the build.

## Punctuation
- No em dashes, en dashes used as dashes, or spaced hyphens used as dashes. Use commas, colons, periods or parentheses instead.
- No half-sentences or sentence fragments for emphasis.

## Calls to Action and Buttons
Every call to action, button and instruction names the action or the outcome.

**Good:** "Create account," "Download the report," "Book a 30-minute call," "Retry upload," "Save and continue," "Next: Choose your workspace," "Skip for now."

**Banned:** "Get Started," "Learn More," "Click Here," "Submit," "Continue" (without context), "Explore."

**Exception:** provider sign-in buttons use the provider's required wording (such as "Continue with Google").

## Messages
- Every info and error message says what happened and the specific next step, in plain language.
- Any button in a message names its action.
- Banned: "Something went wrong," "An error occurred" or similar without a cause and next step.
- Never show raw error codes or stack traces.
- Exception: sign-in, sign-up and password reset use the generic messages in `accounts-and-onboarding.md` so they never reveal whether an account exists.

## Content Volume
- Include only as much content in each section as is necessary.
- Use three items only when the real content has exactly three. Never pad or trim content to reach three.

## Truthfulness
- All content must be truthful about the site.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, user counts, client logos, statistics or other unsupported claims.
- When real content isn't available, use clearly marked placeholders (such as "[Customer quote]"), and list every one under "Open content placeholders" in the delivery summary.
- Never use placeholders in public machine-readable data (JSON-LD, Open Graph, security.txt). Leave unconfirmed fields out.

## Clichés
Never use these or close variations.

**Verbs:** unlock, elevate, supercharge, empower, unleash, harness, leverage, revolutionize, transform, streamline, delve, dive in.

**Adjectives:** seamless, effortless, cutting-edge, next-level, game-changing, best-in-class, world-class, robust, powerful, intuitive.

**Phrases:** "In today's fast-paced world," "It's not just X, it's Y," "Whether you're X or Y," "Look no further," "Say goodbye to X," "Take X to the next level," "The future of X," "Designed with you in mind," "Your all-in-one X," "Imagine a world where," "At the heart of," "Ready to X?"
